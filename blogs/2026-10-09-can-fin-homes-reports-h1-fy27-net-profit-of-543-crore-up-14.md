Can Fin Homes reported strong H1 FY27 results, with net profit expanding by 14% YoY to ₹543 crore. Loan assets rose steadily to ₹43,539 crore, supported by half-yearly disbursements growing 9% to ₹4,982 crore. Q2 operations drove revenue to ₹1,130 crore, representing ≈8.65% growth.
