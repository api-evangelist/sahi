ideaForge has concluded a definitive loan deal for ₹151 cr with the TDB, under the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India. Financing the ₹302 cr Project Yeti at a nominal 4% simple interest rate ensures deep-tech R&D continuation while entirely shielding equity investors from capital dilution.
