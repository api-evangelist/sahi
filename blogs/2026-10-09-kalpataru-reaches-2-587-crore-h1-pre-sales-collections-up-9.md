Kalpataru Limited delivered a steady H1 FY27 performance with pre-sales holding flat at ₹2,587 crore, while sales collections expanded by 9% YoY to ₹2,554 crore. The developer strategically expanded its premium footprint in the Mumbai Metropolitan Region (MMR) through its newly launched project in Goregaon.
