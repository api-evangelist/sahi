Karamtara Engineering delivered robust top-line momentum in Q1 FY27, with consolidated revenue rising 72.3% YoY to ₹1,581 crore and consolidated net profit growing 45.3% YoY to ₹87.37 crore. The performance was largely propelled by renewable energy products, which contributed 82% of the overall revenue, and strong export markets making up 66% of total sales. However, EBITDA margins experienced contraction due to freight and logistical execution costs.
