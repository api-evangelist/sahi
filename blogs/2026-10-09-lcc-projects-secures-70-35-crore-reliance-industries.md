The recently listed EPC firm LCC Projects has bagged substation contracts worth ₹70.35 crore from Reliance Industries. This comes alongside its Q1 FY27 financial results reporting a consolidated net profit of ₹65.76 crore, with a robust order book of ₹8,387.80 crore providing strong long-term revenue visibility.
