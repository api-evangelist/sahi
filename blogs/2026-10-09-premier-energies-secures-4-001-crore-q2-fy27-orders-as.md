Premier Energies secured fresh orders worth ₹4,001 crore in Q2 FY 2027, covering 2,308 MW of solar cell and module supplies. Alongside these commercial wins, the company has successfully expanded its module manufacturing capacity to 11.1 GW and solar cell capacity to 10.6 GW, solidifying its position as India's largest integrated solar manufacturer.
