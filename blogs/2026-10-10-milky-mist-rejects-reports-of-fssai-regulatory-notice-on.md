Milky Mist Dairy Food Limited explicitly denied media reports suggesting it received a regulatory notice from the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India (FSSAI) or Tamil Nadu Food Safety Department concerning its paneer products. The company submitted a voluntary filing to BSE and NSE stating no official communication or order directing an inquiry has been issued.
