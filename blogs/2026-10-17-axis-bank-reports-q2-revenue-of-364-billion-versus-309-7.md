Axis Bank generated ₹364 billion in Q2 revenue, up from ₹309.7 billion YoY, demonstrating steady top-line growth. Net interest income expanded to ₹15,371 crore alongside a 38% YoY surge in net profit to ₹7,003 crore and asset quality stabilization.
