AXISCADES Technologies has formed a 100% wholly owned subsidiary, Xponential Space Private Limited, with an initial share capital subscription of ₹1 lakh. The entity marks a strategic expansion into satellite manufacturing, mission management, space data analytics, and space infrastructure.
