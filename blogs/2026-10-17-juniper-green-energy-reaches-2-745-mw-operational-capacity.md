Juniper Green Energy continues to expand its operational clean energy portfolio across utility-scale solar, wind, hybrid, and BESS assets. Grounded exchange disclosures verify recent project commissionings, including a 150 MW hybrid power project and 15.5 MW FDRE wind additions. In Q1 FY27, the company delivered strong financial execution, generating ₹324 crore in total income with a 91% EBITDA margin.
