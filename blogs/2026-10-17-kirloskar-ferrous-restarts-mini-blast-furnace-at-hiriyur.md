Kirloskar Ferrous Industries completed scheduled maintenance at its Hiriyur plant on schedule, restarting Mini Blast Furnace operations on October 16, 2026. The operational resumption restores pig iron production capacity, aligning with steady operational performance shown in Q1 FY27 revenue of ₹1,771.5 cr.
