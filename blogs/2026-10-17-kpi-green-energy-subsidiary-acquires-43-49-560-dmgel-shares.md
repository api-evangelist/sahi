KPI Green Energy Limited's material subsidiary, Sun Drops Energia Limited, has expanded its equity stake in DEK and Mavericks Green Energy Limited to 88.37% after acquiring an additional 43,49,560 equity shares. The transaction builds on its earlier initial acquisition of a 62.91% controlling stake completed in September 2026.
