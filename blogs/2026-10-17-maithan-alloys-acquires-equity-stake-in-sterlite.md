Maithan Alloys executed an open-market equity acquisition in Sterlite Technologies. While the alert specifies a 0.04% equity holding for ₹19.97 crore (as stated in the source alert; not independently verified), BSE and NSE disclosures confirm Maithan Alloys' ongoing strategy of making minority financial investments in listed equities.
