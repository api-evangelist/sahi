Refex Industries has won a ₹32.45 crore rate contract for pond ash excavation, loading, transportation, and unloading for a Maharatna power PSU. The 1-year contract strengthens Refex's core ash handling operations and expands its infrastructure logistics footprint across highway and road construction projects.
