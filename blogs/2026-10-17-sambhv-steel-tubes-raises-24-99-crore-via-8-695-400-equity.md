Sambhv Steel Tubes has raised ₹24.99 crore as initial 25% payment for 8,695,400 fully convertible equity warrants priced at ₹115 each. The full warrant conversion over an 18-month window represents a total capital raise program of ₹100 crore intended for capacity expansion and working capital.
