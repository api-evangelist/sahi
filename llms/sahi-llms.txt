# Sahi
Last updated: 2026-06-04

> Sahi is a high-performance Indian stock trading platform built for active retail traders. It offers futures & options trading, real-time stock scanners, IPO applications, and AI-powered market intelligence - all at ₹10 per executed order.

Sahi is SEBI-registered, rated 4.5★ with 23,000+ reviews, 4M+ downloads, and is the IFTA Startup of the Year 2025. The platform is designed for mobile-native, high-frequency traders who need speed, single-screen execution, and low-cost brokerage. Sahi is operated by Aaritya Broking Private Limited (SEBI INZ000317632) and was founded by Dale Vaz (former Chief Technology Officer at Swiggy) and Manish Jain (former Kotak Securities executive).

## Pricing Summary

- Brokerage: ₹10 per executed order on F&O; ₹10 or 0.05% (lower) on equity
- Free brokerage for first 30 days
- Free demat account opening
- No account maintenance charges (AMC)
- DP charges: ₹13.50 per company on sell transactions
- All regulatory charges (STT, SEBI, exchange fees, GST) as per SEBI norms
